Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add students? +
Go to the Student Management dashboard and click "Add Student". You can enter details manually or upload a CSV file.
Can students register themselves? +
Yes. In the Course Management section, open registration for the course and share the generated link with students. They can register themselves using the link.
How can I compile results? +
Ensure all students are enrolled in the course. Then go to the Result Dashboard, enter scores, and click "Compile". You can then print or save the final result sheet.
Can I close course registration? +
Yes. After all students have registered, simply toggle the course status to "Closed" in Course Management.
Is there a way to print the class list? +
Yes. After students are enrolled, go to the course page and click "Print List" to download the class list as a PDF.
How do I print results? +
After compiling the results in the Result Dashboard, navigate to the Result Management Dashboard. Click on the three-dot icon next to the relevant course to open the options menu. From the menu, select “Generate Result”. A printable version of the compiled result sheet will be generated, which you can either download as a PDF or print directly.
