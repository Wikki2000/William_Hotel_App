Getting Started with WIS Grader

1
Register and Log In

Start by creating an account or logging in to your existing one.

2
Add Students

In the Student Management dashboard, you can:

  • Manually add students one by one
  • Manage and edit student records
3
Add Courses & Enroll Students

In the Course Management section, you can:

  • Create new courses
  • Enable course registration and share a link with students (e.g., via course rep)
  • Students who register via the link will be automatically enrolled in the course
  • Close registration when done to prevent further entries
  • You can also enroll students manually
After registration, print the list of enrolled students for attendance and confirmation before compiling results.
4
Manage and Compile Results

In the Result Dashboard, you can:

  • Create a result entry for each course
  • Ensure all students are properly enrolled before compiling results
  • Enter or upload scores and compile the results based on your grading setup
  • Generate printable or downloadable result files
